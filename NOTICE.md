## Trusted root certificates

A TLS client validates the server's certificate against a root store
compiled into the binary. It is the Mozilla CA root set as extracted by
curl (<https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html>), converted to a lookup table
by `tools/gen_ca_roots.sh`; curl distributes that bundle under the
[MPL-2.0](https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/), and the page above is where
its source form lives. The certificates themselves belong to the
certificate authorities that issued them.

## Unicode property data

The regular-expression property tables hold the general categories,
scripts and binary properties of Unicode 16.0, generated by
`tools/gen_unicode_props.mjs`.
